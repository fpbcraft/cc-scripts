THE SHATTERED EXPANSE v4.0 (TIDEBOUND)

INSTALL:
  wget run https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fpbcraft/cc-scripts/main/install-tidebound.lua

PLAY:
  /tidebound/tidebound.lua

1. Choose 1-4 human empires. AI plays every remaining empire.
2. Pass the monitor to each human on the Begin Turn screen.
3. Tap a star, then SYSTEM ORDERS. Tap unavailable actions for explanations.
4. Ships > ORDER > destination (connected star) to move the selected ship.
5. Science ships reveal systems; builders establish outposts and colonies.
6. Research and buildings cost accumulated resources, not empire actions.
7. Each ship has individual orders, refreshed every galactic year.
8. Pick narrative decisions and diplomatic replies before ending your turn.
9. Win by military conquest, research, diplomacy, economics or Legacy score.

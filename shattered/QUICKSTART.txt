SHATTERED V5: THE SHATTERED EXPANSE

INSTALL:
wget run https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fpbcraft/cc-scripts/main/install-shattered.lua

PLAY:
/shattered/shattered.lua

HARDWARE:
Advanced Computer + Advanced Monitor (5x5 recommended); CC: Graphics optional.

THE TURN:
Select a star to inspect. Ships -> ORDER -> linked star -> Confirm.
Scouts reveal a system. Ark -> found an adjacent surveyed neutral system.
Worlds sets Industry / Science / Commerce for each colony.
Tech spends Knowledge, with no empire action cap. Fleet gets 2 moves/year.
Diplomacy creates pacts, alliance and war. Council events change your story.
END advances to the next player and runs AI turns automatically.

VICTORY:
Discovery (explore + ancient clues), Unity (alliances), Dominion (15 systems)
or highest Legacy score at the end of the campaign.

SAVES: /shattered/v5-save.dat (v4 and v3 saves retained separately)
OLD V4: /shattered/shattered-shattered-v4.lua

RAILBREAK: KINETIC FRONTIER v2 - TRAIN-FIRST EDITION
=====================================================

UPDATE: wget run https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fpbcraft/cc-scripts/main/install-railbreak.lua
PLAY:   /railbreak/main.lua
OLD FACTORY EDITION: /railbreak/classic.lua

Connect an Advanced Monitor (3x3 through 5x5). If multiple screens
are attached, the largest will be used by default. Select one with:
  /railbreak/main.lua monitor_0

HOW TO WIN
1. Use station MARKET to buy planks, ore, parts or fuel.
2. Take CONTRACTS at their origin stations.
3. Choose connected destinations on the MAP, pay track and fuel costs.
4. Choose an event solution if the train is interrupted.
5. Deliver cargo at the right station to earn cash and reputation.
6. Complete at least 2 jobs / earn 2 reputation and arrive at EASTPORT.

TRAIN: Tap wagons to inspect, then ADD / LV UP / TECH.
TOOLTIPS: Tap ? for screen help, icons for resource help, or an item
  to see its benefits, costs and requirements. Choose BACK (no cost)
  or CONFIRM. PREV / NEXT pages show longer descriptions.
STATION: Repair, refuel, upgrade modules and run turn-based shifts.
CREW: Optional P1/P2/P3/P4 attribution toggle; gameplay is shared.
HELP: Includes main menu access. The game autosaves after successful actions.

SAVES: /railbreak/data/train-save.lua (v2) vs /railbreak/data/save.lua (old)
No timers govern gameplay. Animation is cosmetic only.
